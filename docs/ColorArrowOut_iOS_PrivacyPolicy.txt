2. SCOPE — WHAT THIS POLICY COVERS (iOS BUILD)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This document describes data practices for the iOS version of ColorArrow Out
based on the App’s current implementation:

  • Local device storage for gameplay and settings
  • Optional local (on-device) notifications
  • Third-party advertising via Google AdMob
  • Optional links to the Apple App Store for rating the App

IMPORTANT — Firebase is NOT used on iOS:

  (no GoogleService-Info.plist). In-app analytics collection is disabled.
  We do not send gameplay analytics events to Firebase from the iOS App.

3. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ColorArrow Out is primarily an offline puzzle game. We do not require you to
create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone
number, payment card details, or other personal registration information to
play.

3.1 Information stored on your device (locally)
    The App may store the following on your device using on-device storage
    (for example Apple UserDefaults / SharedPreferences):

    • Game progress (unlocked levels, stars / completion status)
    • Settings preferences (sound, music, haptics / vibration, color theme)
    • App review prompt flags (for example launch count and “don’t ask again”)
    • Local notification schedule fingerprint (to avoid rescheduling the same
      offline reminder set unnecessarily)

    This data stays on your device. We do not operate our own server that
    receives or stores this gameplay data from the iOS App.

3.2 Local notifications (on-device only)
    If you grant notification permission, the App may schedule local reminders
    on your device (for example daily play reminders). These notifications:

    • Are generated and delivered on your device
    • Do not use Apple Push Notification service (APNs) remote push from our
      servers
    • Do not use Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)
    • Work without an internet connection once scheduled

    You can deny or revoke notification permission in iOS Settings at any time.

3.3 Information collected by advertising partners (Google AdMob)
    The App displays ads through Google AdMob (Google LLC) to keep the App
    free to play. When ads are requested (typically only when the device has
    network connectivity), Google and its advertising partners may collect
    or process certain information from your device, which may include:

    • Advertising identifiers (for example Identifier for Advertisers / IDFA,
      where permitted by Apple and your choices)
    • IP address
    • Device type, operating system version, and app version
    • Approximate location derived from IP address (not precise GPS from us)
    • Ad performance and interaction data (for example impressions and clicks)
    • Diagnostic information related to ad delivery

    This information is collected and processed by Google under Google’s own
    privacy policies. We do not control how Google uses this data.

    Google Privacy Policy:
      https://policies.google.com/privacy
    How Google uses information from sites or apps that use Google services:
      https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
    Google’s advertising / AdMob information:
      https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

3.4 Network connectivity check
    The App may check whether your device appears online (for example via
    system network / connectivity status) so that ads are not requested while
    offline. This check is used for App functionality and does not by itself
    create an account or upload your puzzle progress to our servers.

3.5 App Store rating / store links
    The App may show an optional in-app rating prompt and may open the Apple
    App Store listing (using Apple’s review APIs or App Store links). Apple
    processes any rating or review you submit under Apple’s terms and privacy
    policy. We do not receive the content of your App Store review through
    our own servers.

3.6 Information we do NOT collect on iOS (current build)
    For the current iOS App we do not:

    • Use Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, or Firebase Cloud Messaging
    • Require sign-in or collect account credentials
    • Collect precise GPS location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone,
      health, or motion fitness data for our own features
    • Sell your personal information

7. TRACKING AND APP TRACKING TRANSPARENCY (ATT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

On iOS, personalized advertising that uses data to track you across other
companies’ apps and websites may require Apple’s App Tracking Transparency
permission, depending on how ads are configured and which identifiers are
used.

    • If the App requests tracking permission, you can Allow or Ask App Not
      to Track.
    • You can also change this later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security →
      Tracking.
    • You can limit Ad Tracking / personalized ads using Apple’s advertising
      settings on your device.

Even if you decline tracking, non-personalized ads may still be shown so the
App can remain free.
